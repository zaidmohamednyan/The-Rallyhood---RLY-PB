About Rallyhood

A club built around the people between the points.

We believe the best pickleball community is equal parts sport, belonging and good organisation. Rallyhood RLY Empire brings players together through accessible sessions, spirited competition and thoughtfully run events.

Whether you are holding a paddle for the first time or hunting for the next podium, there is room on court.
